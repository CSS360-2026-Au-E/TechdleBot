# SBOM List
To see the SBOM list, download the dependency-radar.html file and open it locally. It will contain a webpage with a list of 198 dependencies, both direct and transitive, as well
as runtime and developer, along with a plethora of other data, metadata, and ways of displaying that data. Clicking on this [link](dependency-radar.html) will take you 
directly to the relevant file, where all you have to do is download it by clicking the down arrow icon in the top right.
